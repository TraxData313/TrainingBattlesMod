- [x] Playtest round 2 build - one real BUG squashed, two papercuts, one NEW FEATURE. (1) THE UPGRADE-XP BUG (Anton: 15 battle sisters, 8 waiting upgrades melting to 6 after a drill; other troops losing waiting upgrades too). Root cause found in the game's own code, not ours: PartyBase.OnXpChanged CLAMPS every troop stack's XP to (men in stack x max upgrade cost) on every XP write - so when men die in battle the stack shrinks and the next XP write silently destroys the dead men's stored upgrade progress. Our aftermath restored the MEN but scaled XP by delta arithmetic, which cannot see clamped-away XP. Fix, two-pronged: at SPLIT time the men now carry their proportional share of stack XP across to the opponent party (vanilla party-screen behavior; also keeps both halves off their clamp ceilings so battle earnings are not eaten), and the AFTERMATH now restores XP ABSOLUTELY - men come back first (raising the clamp ceiling to full), then each stack's XP is SET to its pre-battle pool plus the kept share of visibly-earned XP (target = beforeXp + XpKept(max(0, after-before))). Conservative by construction: clamp losses mid-battle count as unearned, never as punishment. (2) Default hotkey T -> G (T is the vanilla combat log key); ConfigVersion bumped to 2 with a migration that moves configs still carrying the old T default to G while honoring hand-picked keys; MCM dropdown reordered, ParseKey fallback updated.
